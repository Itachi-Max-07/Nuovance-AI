## 1. Introduction and Scope

Nuovance AI is an AI automation and software engineering agency. We build AI voice agents, workflow automation on n8n, custom software, UGC video and content production systems, and data pipelines and integrations for our clients.

This policy covers:

- **nuovanceai.com** — every page, form, and booking link on it.
- **Prospect and client communication** — email, WhatsApp, phone, and consultation bookings.
- **Systems we build and operate for clients**, where those systems process personal data on the client's behalf.
- **Authorized platform integrations** — LinkedIn, Google, Meta, X, and other OAuth providers that you explicitly connect.

This policy does **not** cover third-party websites we link to, our booking provider's own pages, our clients' own products and privacy practices, or the social platforms themselves. Each of those is governed by its own privacy policy.

### Controller and Processor — Which One We Are

The distinction decides who you send a request to.

| Situation | Our role | What that means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You visit nuovanceai.com, submit a form, book a consultation, or email us | **Controller** | We decide why and how your data is used. Send rights requests directly to us. |
| You are a client, and we process data inside a system we built for you | **Processor** | You are the controller. We act only on your documented instructions. |
| You are an end user of a client's product that we operate | **Processor** | Send your request to that client. If you send it to us, we will forward it to them without undue delay and tell you we have done so. |
| You connect your own LinkedIn, Google, Meta, or X account to a Nuovance AI feature | **Controller** for the connection itself | We decide how the token and returned data are handled, within the limits set out in Sections 4 and 6. |

Where we act as a processor, our obligations are fixed by the written agreement we have with that client rather than by this policy. If you are a client and need a separate data processing agreement, email nuovanceaiagency@gmail.com and we will negotiate one with you. We do not maintain a standard template — each agreement is drafted for the engagement.

## 6. Limited Use and Platform Data Restrictions

These commitments apply to all data received from LinkedIn, Google, Meta, X, and every other OAuth provider. They are absolute and are not subject to a "unless we tell you otherwise" exception.

1. **We do not sell, rent, or license platform data.** Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to information resellers, not to anyone — for money or for any other consideration.

2. **We do not transfer platform data except to deliver the feature you asked for.** The only transfers are to the subprocessors listed in [Section 8](#8-how-we-share-your-information) that are strictly necessary to provide the user-facing feature, or where you direct it, or where the law compels it. Where any other workflow sends content to an AI provider, how that provider handles it is set out in [Section 7](#7-ai-and-automated-processing).

4. **No human at Nuovance AI reads your platform data**, with three exceptions, each of them narrow:
   - **With your consent** — for example when you ask us to debug a specific automation and agree to us looking at the data involved.
   - **For security** — investigating a suspected breach, abuse, or fraud affecting our systems or yours.
   - **Where the law requires it** — a valid legal order, described in [Section 8](#8-how-we-share-your-information).

5. **We do not use platform data for advertising.** Not for targeting, not for measurement, not for building audiences or lookalike segments, and not for any interest-based profile.

6. **We collect the minimum.** We request the narrowest scopes that make a feature work, and drop a scope at the next authorization when the feature that needed it is removed.

**Google-specific.** Nuovance AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Meta-specific.** We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies, including the restrictions on selling, licensing, and purchasing Platform Data, and the prohibition on using Platform Data to build or augment user profiles for advertising. Our application accesses only Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts that Nuovance AI owns and operates, it does not collect or store the personal data of other Facebook or Instagram users, and no Meta Platform Data is sent to any third-party AI provider.

**X-specific.** We comply with the X Developer Agreement and Policy, including its restrictions on redistributing X Content, its off-platform matching restrictions, and its requirement that deleted, protected, or suspended content be honoured in our systems within 24 hours.

If a platform's terms and this policy ever disagree, the platform's terms govern that platform's data and we will amend this policy.

## 11. Security Measures

These are the controls we operate. We do not claim any certification or audit we do not hold.

**Encryption**

- Traffic to nuovanceai.com is served over TLS 1.2 or higher, with HSTS enabled and a `max-age` of two years including subdomains.
- A strict Content Security Policy is enforced site-wide, along with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, and a restrictive Permissions-Policy.
- Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting and database providers under their own standard encryption.
- OAuth and Page access tokens are stored securely, are never exposed in client-side code, and are never committed to source control.

**Access control**

- Access is granted on least privilege: each person gets the narrowest access their role needs, and nothing beyond it.
- Multi-factor authentication is used on accounts that support it.
- Production credentials are kept out of source control and are not exposed in client-side code.
- Access is removed when someone leaves or changes role.
- Client production data is accessed only when required to deliver or support the engagement, and only by personnel assigned to it.

**Operational**

- Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities and updated when an issue is found.
- Backups are encrypted.
- Personnel are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

**Incident response**

If a personal data breach occurs, we will:

1. Contain it and assess the scope and risk without delay.
2. Notify the relevant supervisory authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware, where GDPR Article 33 requires it.
3. Notify the Indian Data Protection Board and every affected Data Principal as required by Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act 2023, without delay and in the form the Rules prescribe.
4. Notify CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing the incident, where the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022 require it.
5. Tell affected individuals directly, in plain language, where the risk to them is high.
6. Tell affected clients without undue delay so they can meet their own notification duties.

No system is perfectly secure. We do not promise that ours is — we promise these controls, and honest, prompt disclosure if they fail.
